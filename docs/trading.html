Simulated trading & portfolio

Every trade on this page is simulated. The engine replays the Kalshi demo candlestick history (backtest) and then trades forward on every live collection cycle, marking positions to the live bid and settling them when a market resolves yes or no. No real order has been placed against a Kalshi account — see Flagged for review for why.

Execution assumptions

  • Single book. Kalshi publishes YES bids and NO bids only; a YES bid at $0.07 is the same level as a NO ask at $0.93. A YES position is bought at the YES ask and closed at the YES bid; a NO position at the NO ask and closed at the NO bid.
  • Backtest fills use the 1-minute candle close ± $0.01 as a conservative bid/ask proxy.
  • Forward paper fills use the live orderbook touch captured by the collector.
  • Position sizing is a fixed dollar risk per trade (see the strategy table), capped by contracts and by resting size.
  • Resolution. When Kalshi reports a market result of yes or no, open positions settle at $1 (winning side) or $0 (losing side) and the resolution is recorded on the trade.
  • No real orders. The signed live-order client exists in pipeline/src/live-trade.js but is inert without a Kalshi API key, and none could be provisioned here.
