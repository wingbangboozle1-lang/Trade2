Gas prices on Kalshi demo

Retail fuel and crude price contracts: average US gasoline thresholds (for example the KXAAAGASM series) and WTI front-month settlement targets (KXWTIMAX). Prices are quoted in dollars per contract — each contract pays $1 if the outcome happens.

How this topic is tracked

  • Identification: Kalshi series whose title or ticker mentions gas, gasoline, diesel, fuel, crude oil, WTI or Brent. The Kalshi-reported category is recorded but never edited.
  • Dollar amount = open interest (contracts) × current price — the notional that would change hands if every contract settled at the current price.
  • Most gas contracts here settle on a daily or monthly cadence, so the hour-to-close column is short and swings are common.
