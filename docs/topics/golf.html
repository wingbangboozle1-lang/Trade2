Golf on Kalshi demo

Tour events, majors, match play and player props — tournament winners, cut lines and round-level markets. Golf contracts close quickly, which makes them the cleanest live test of the alert system: a leader changes a price within minutes of the last hole.

How this topic is tracked

  • Identification: series whose title or ticker mentions golf, PGA, Masters, Ryder Cup, US Open, Open Championship, LPGA or player names attached to a tournament.
  • Short-dated contracts mean the "time to close" component of the ROI model scores them highly when they are also liquid.
  • Prices follow the tournament: the swing feed for golf is usually the fastest-moving of the five topics.
