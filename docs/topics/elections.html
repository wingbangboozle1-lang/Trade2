Elections on Kalshi demo

Election winners, nominations, appointments, referendums and ballot outcomes — the largest dollar book on the demo exchange. Most of these contracts resolve far in the future, so their prices move on polling, procedure and headline news rather than on a game-day clock.

How this topic is tracked

  • Identification: series whose title or ticker mentions elections, president, senate, house, congress, primary, governor, mayor, nominee, referendum or a named officeholder.
  • These are long-dated contracts: the ROI model deliberately scores them lower on the "time to close" component because a single headline can move them a long way.
  • Each market links to the official settlement sources Kalshi publishes for it (election offices, AP, Reuters and similar).
