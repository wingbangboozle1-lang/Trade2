2 · The $0.10 swing rule

  • Threshold. A swing is a change of at least $0.10 (10 cents) in the YES contract price, in either direction. The comparison is done in integer cents, so a move of exactly $0.10 qualifies and a move of $0.09 does not.
  • Reference 1 — consecutive fills. The collector pulls GET /markets/trades and compares each fill with the previous fill on the same market. The alert keeps both prices, the trade size and the exact timestamp of the print.
  • Reference 2 — Kalshi's previous price. Every market response carries previous_price_dollars; when that field differs from the current price by $0.10 or more, an alert is raised with the reference labelled Kalshi previous_price_dollars. A previous price of $0.00 is treated as "no previous print" and is not used.
  • Noise control. Alerts are de-duplicated per ticker, direction and minute, and the log keeps the 400 most recent swings.
  • Attribution. An alert never claims to know what caused the move. It links the Kalshi market page and the official settlement sources Kalshi publishes for that event, so a trader can check them in order.

3 · ROI model specification

The model produces a 0–100 score plus the reasons behind it. It is a transparent heuristic, not a prediction guarantee.

ComponentRangeDefinitionInputs
Momentum0–30Size of the most recent move against the price one hour ago (or the last recorded reference): 30 points at a $0.10 move, scaled linearly to 0price history, trade feed
Liquidity0–25Log-scaled size resting within 5¢ of the mid, from ~10 to ~3,000 contractsorderbook
Spread0–15Cost of crossing the book: 15 points at a $0.01 spread, 0 at $0.10 or widerorderbook
Edge0–15Distance of the last price from its own 30-minute mean: 15 points at $0.10 away, 0 at the meanprice history
Time0–15Hours to close: full marks between 1 and 72 hours, decaying to 6 points beyond a year, 4 points under an hourmarket close time
Fair value0–1mean-reversion pull (50% of the gap to the 30-minute mean) blended with momentum persistence (30% of the last move), damped by 20%price history
Expected value±$fair YES − last price (YES side) and its negative (NO side); shown per contractfair value
Model risk: the score is a ranking aid built from public market data. It has no access to news, opinion polls or settlement information, and it is not investment advice.

4 · Execution assumptions (simulated trading)

  • Single book. Kalshi publishes YES bids and NO bids only; a YES bid at $0.07 is the same level as a NO ask at $0.93. YES positions are entered at the YES ask and closed at the YES bid; NO positions at the NO ask and closed at the NO bid.
  • Backtest fills use the hourly candle close for the reference price and the candle's own bid/ask closes for entry and exit.
  • Forward paper fills use the live orderbook touch captured during collection.
  • Position sizing is a fixed dollar risk per trade ($50 momentum and mean reversion, $25 liquidity capture), capped at 100 / 100 / 50 contracts and never at a price below $0.02 or above $0.98.
  • Resolution. A position is settled when Kalshi reports the market's result; the winning side pays $1 per contract, the losing side $0. The recorded resolution field on the trade states whether the event resolved yes or no.
  • Fees. No fee is applied in the simulation. Kalshi charges a fee on some series; the published fee schedule is not part of the demo API response used here, so this is listed as an unverified simplification on the flagged page.
