Price, spread and depth

Kalshi returns bids only. Arrays are ascending, so the best bid is the last element. The implied YES ask is 1.00 − best NO bid and the YES mid is the average of the two. Contract depth is the sum of count_fp at every resting price level, reported as contracts available at each price.

Swing threshold

An alert is raised when the YES mid moves by at least $0.10 between two consecutive observations of the same market. Missing quotes are skipped, never treated as zero. A $0.10 move on a $1 contract is 10 cents of settlement value.

Fees

Simulated fills use Kalshi's published taker fee, ceil(M × 0.07 × C × P × (1 − P)) to the cent, from the official fee schedule. All 42 published rows of that table are reproduced exactly by the implementation and are asserted in tests/test_engine.py. Fee-free series (multiplier 0) are respected via each series' own fee_multiplier.

The high-ROI filter (order-book model)

The second chat is a selective filter over the same swings. It is priced from the order book only, so that both ends of every comparison are the same kind of number:

Gate 6 matters because the demo feed prints alternating trades on markets whose book never moves: on KXPRESNOMD-28-AKLO the one-minute candles alternate between $0.8030 and $0.0030 while the quoted book stays at $0.0020 × $0.0030 for the whole window (official candles ↗). A price move the book does not confirm is not a market move, so the model passes on it and says why.

Candidate news on a swing

Each swing row can carry candidate headlines found through Google News RSS by keyword and publish time (the feed ↗). They are labelled as candidates on purpose: temporal proximity is not causation, and a headline is never presented as the confirmed cause of a move. Every swing row also links to the exact Kalshi API response it was computed from. When no headline matches the window, the row says so instead of inventing one.

Settlement

Positions settle from the market's official result field: YES pays $1.00 per contract, NO pays nothing, as documented in Market Settlement.

What is deliberately not claimed

  • A headline near a move is a candidate, never a confirmed cause. The alert always links to the official Kalshi market response as well.
  • The paper ledger is not your demo account balance. Authenticated portfolio endpoints require credentials this repo does not hold.
  • Unadjusted open interest is not traded dollar volume.
  • The model's fair value is a rule, not a forecast of where a price will settle.

Every published row links to the exact official response it came from. The scheduled pipeline re-opens those responses and compares them with what this site shows, one market at a time.
